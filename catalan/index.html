CATALAN NUMBERS & PRIMES

Cn = (2n)! / ((n+1)! · n!) — BALLOT PATHS · PARENTHESIZATIONS · BINARY TREES

FORMULA

Cn = (2n)! / ((n+1)! · n!)
Cn = C(2n, n) / (n+1)
Also: Cn+1 = Σ Ci·Cn-i for i=0..n

ASYMPTOTIC GROWTH

Cn ~ 4n / (n3/2 · √π)
Grows exponentially with base 4

PRIME INDEX HIGHLIGHTS

When n is prime, Cn has special divisibility properties.
C2=2, C3=5, C5=42, C7=429

CATALAN PRIMES

Catalan numbers that are themselves prime:
C2 = 2 and C3 = 5
No other Cn for n≤10 is prime.
