Kummer's Theorem: The highest power of prime p dividing C(m+n, m) equals the number of carries when adding m and n in base p. Thus C(n,k) ≡ 0 (mod p) iff there is at least one carry when adding k and (n−k) in base p. This produces the Sierpinski-like fractal pattern: a self-similar structure where each "triangle" is replaced by p(p+1)/2 smaller copies.
